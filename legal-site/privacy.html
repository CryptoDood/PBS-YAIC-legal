1. Who this policy covers

This policy covers YAIC, a product of Prime Business Solutions LLC ("PBS"). PBS incubates YAIC and related products; as products graduate from the PBS incubator, operating or licensing ownership may transfer to a downstream entity (for example, Nexus Prime) under a documented agreement. This policy describes PBS's current data-handling practices for YAIC regardless of that future ownership path.

2. What data we process

  • Case and workflow data: checklist status, document metadata, conversation content, and the canonical state of a customer's case with a business using YAIC ("tenant").
  • Documents: identity, financial, or other regulated documents a tenant's workflow requires, classified at intake and handled per that classification.
  • Messages: content sent and received across connected channels, preserved in original language alongside any translation.
  • Staff/operator data: the identity of tenant staff who act on cases, explicitly linked by that staff member, never inferred.
  • Channel-provider metadata: whatever a connected channel itself provides when relaying a message — message IDs, timestamps, sender identifiers on that channel.

3. Tenant isolation

Every tenant's data is isolated from every other tenant's by architecture. No tenant's credentials, case data, or staff identity mappings are reachable from another tenant's request context.

4. Data minimization and classification

Data is classified at intake (identity, financial, regulated, or general) and that classification governs storage, retention, and access. Where a workflow allows, document content defaults to pass-through: we validate against policy and extract only the specific fields the workflow needs. We do not collect what the workflow does not need.

5. Retention and deletion

Retention is configured per tenant per document class. Deleting content removes or irreversibly redacts it from primary and backup storage; our audit trail keeps the fact that an action occurred without retaining the deleted content itself. Deletion propagates to backups within a bounded time.

6. Multilingual handling

Where a tenant and their customer work in different languages, we preserve the original-language content alongside any translation — translation never silently alters canonical facts (names, dates, identifiers, monetary values, document identity, or checklist state).

7. Logging

Operational logs never carry message content, contact details, caller identity strings, third-party provider response text, or credentials.

8. Connected providers

Depending on which channels and integrations a tenant configures, the following providers may process data strictly to deliver that tenant's own messages/data through that channel: Meta / WhatsApp Business Platform, Slack Technologies, Telegram, Discord, LinkedIn (where configured), Google Cloud Platform, Google Workspace, and the tenant's own connected CRM.
